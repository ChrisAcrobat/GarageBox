Strum spread

Sweep line and timing for notes struck one after another (F17). A strum down goes green → orange (low to high), a strum up orange → green (F17.1); each fret flashes when the line reaches it (F17.2).

Draft blueprint for F17. Not wired into the app and not a current requirement; the lane here is a simple stand-in. Click a fret to hold or release it — only held frets take part in the strum. Open points: Q5 (what to show at 0 ms), Q7 (default strum time).

At 0 ms (Q5, open):
Keys: ↓ strum down, ↑ strum up, 1–5 hold/release a fret.

Timing follows design 15: the held notes are spread evenly from the first to the last over the strum time, so with n held frets note i starts at i / (n − 1) × strum time. A new strum while one is still passing drops the notes the old one has not reached (F17.5).
