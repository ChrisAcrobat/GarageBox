# 71. Menu hints and Swedish terms

**Decision:** **Hints: one line at the bottom of the settings panel (guitar text also mentions that frets play directly), one in the device information view, and "Controller: press Start." on the start screen; all in `en.json`/`sv.json` (Swedish: "styrkors" for D-pad, "slag" for strum, "greppknappar" for frets).**

**Why:** Discoverability without a help page.

**Alternatives:** No hints.

**Where:** `lang/*.json`.

Milestone: Gamepad menu navigation

Importance: 5 – Visible wording, easy to adjust.
