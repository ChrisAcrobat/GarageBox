# 17. Per-device "Reset to defaults" button

**Decision:** **Per-device "Reset to defaults" button** in the settings panel.

**Why:** Cheap and useful once there are many settings.

**Alternatives:** None.

**Where:** `settings-panel.js`, `store.resetDeviceSettings`.

Milestone: Milestone 2 – guitar sound

Importance: 5 – Small visible convenience feature.

**Update 2026-10-22:** the button now keeps the instrument choice (same level as the 5 s Back hold, R4.11.2); see `4-reset-button-keeps-instrument.md`.
