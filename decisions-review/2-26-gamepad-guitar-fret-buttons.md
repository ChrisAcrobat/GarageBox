# 26. Gamepad guitar/bass fret buttons

**Decision:** **Gamepad guitar/bass frets: A green, B red, Y yellow, X blue, LB orange; D-pad up/down strum** (Back = tilt on/off on a short press, which on a gamepad never moves the range (R4.5.7), and a 5 s hold resets the controller's settings (R4.11.2); Start = settings). Back no longer mutes.

**Why:** Xbox face colors match GH fret colors.

**Alternatives:** A,B,X,Y order.

**Where:** `guitar.js GAMEPAD_GUITAR_CONTROLS`.

Milestone: Milestone 3 – more instruments

Importance: 2 – The basic way to play guitar/bass on a gamepad.
