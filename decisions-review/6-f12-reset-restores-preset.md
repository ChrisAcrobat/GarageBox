# F12: both resets restore the last opened preset

Importance: 6 – future feature wording only.

**Decision:** F12 now names both reset ways (5 s Back hold R4.11.2 and *Reset to defaults*) as restoring the last opened named preset (or the defaults), replacing "by holding Back, if adopted".

**Rationale:** keeps the future presets consistent with the now-adopted hold.

**Alternatives:** the hold always goes to factory defaults.

**Where:** F12.

**Update 2026-10-22 (owner's reset tiers):** F12 now says the 5 s reset and *Reset to defaults* restore the last opened preset but keep the current instrument, while the 10 s full reset always goes to the factory defaults including the detected instrument, ignoring presets.
