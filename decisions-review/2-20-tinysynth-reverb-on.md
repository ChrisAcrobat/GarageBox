# 20. tinysynth reverb left on

**Decision:** **tinysynth reverb left on (library default 0.3)**.

**Why:** Piano and guitar sound much nicer.

**Alternatives:** Off for lower CPU.

**Where:** `midi-source.js takeSynth`.

Milestone: Milestone 3 – more instruments

Importance: 2 – Directly audible on every MIDI sound (piano, MIDI guitar, drums).
